Your quiz library lives on your device. This policy explains what Quiz Invoker stores, what happens when you share a file, and how to remove your data.

Data on your device

Quiz Invoker stores your quizzes, images, notes, study history, settings, and working materials in the app’s storage. Camera, Photos, and Files are used for the materials you choose. Optional Study Reminders use local iOS notifications.

Quiz Invoker does not require an app account or automatically upload your library to the developer. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing with external apps

Add Quizzes prepares the materials you select and instructions for drafting questions. Learn with AI prepares selected questions and instructions for an explanation, step-by-step hints, or a self-test. You can also choose to include recent practice results or your own notes and mistake reasons.

You choose whether to share, copy, or save the prepared content. You choose the receiving app or location, and you can cancel before sending. Quiz Invoker does not run an AI model, provide an AI service, or receive your conversations in external AI apps. An external app may process, store, or upload what you share under its own privacy policy and age requirements. Check the content before sending, especially if it includes names, photos, or school records.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases, subscriptions, and offer-code redemption through your App Store account. Quiz Invoker keeps verified transaction and account identifiers on the device to restore purchases and avoid granting the same capacity twice. Personal backups and portable quiz files exclude those identifiers and subscription caches. Quiz Invoker does not operate a purchase server.

Backups and data retention

A personal backup can contain your saved quiz library, study history, notes, and settings. You choose where to save it. A passcode-protected backup is encrypted. Quiz Invoker cannot recover a forgotten passcode. Ordinary ZIP tools can open a backup created without a passcode.

Saved data stays on the device until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Add Quizzes keeps working materials while a project is open and releases them when you close or replace the project, or successfully import results. Optional copies used to compare sources may be retained temporarily and are cleaned up automatically. iOS device backups may contain app data.

Deleting data and withdrawing permission

In the Library, removing a quiz moves it to Trash. Permanently deleting it from Trash also removes its personal notes and study history. To remove the app’s local data from this device, use Delete App in iOS. Manage any exported files and device backups separately.

You can change Camera and notification permissions in iOS Settings and stop future sharing by choosing not to send a file. If an external app has already received a copy, manage it with that service. Copies outside Quiz Invoker stay under your or the recipient’s control.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, we use your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send to respond to and follow up on your request. The email providers involved handle that correspondence. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

For a privacy question or to request deletion of support correspondence, contact chanshuowu@icloud.com. Please remove private student information from screenshots or files before emailing us.

Younger students

A parent or guardian should help younger students decide what material is appropriate to share and whether an external AI service is suitable. External services have their own age and account requirements.

Website hosting

When you visit the public site hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. Learn more in the GitHub Pages documentation.
