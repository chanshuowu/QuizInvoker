Get help with adding questions, sharing files, or studying your personal quiz library.

Contact support

For help, feedback, or a feature request, email chanshuowu@icloud.com.

For a problem, include your device model, iOS version, Quiz Invoker version, and steps to reproduce it. Find the app version under Settings → Help & Support. Remove private student information from any screenshots or files you choose to send.

Add and review quizzes

Start with your material and check the results before keeping them.

  1. Open Add Quizzes and select materials, such as a photo, PDF, document, or your own notes.
  2. Review the materials and instructions, then tap Share with AI and choose a compatible external AI app to draft the questions.
  3. Share the returned quiz file back to Quiz Invoker, or use Library → Import to open a saved file. For a text response, choose Import → Paste AI Results and paste the complete quiz-results block.
  4. Review First puts new quizzes in Pending for you to check. Accept Directly saves them when there is room. Accepted quizzes are available to study.

When sharing or importing doesn’t work

If your AI app doesn’t appear in the iOS share sheet, save the file to Files and attach it inside that app. If it cannot handle ZIP files, use Copy Prompt, attach the materials manually, and use Paste AI Results for the complete text response. A link to the conversation cannot be imported as quizzes. If you see an import error, keep your file and follow the in-app message.

For explanations, hints, or a self-test on existing questions, use Learn with AI and select questions from your Library. The conversation continues in the external app, which may require its own account or subscription.

Study offline

Tap Study to practice accepted quizzes offline. Try answering first, then show the answer and choose Correct or Try Again. You can pause and resume later. To choose which questions to focus on, use Study Focus in the Library.

For more walkthroughs, open Settings → Getting Started.

Back up or restore your data

Use Settings → Transfer & Backup → Create Backup to save a copy of your Library and learning data. Export Pending quizzes separately. Restore from Backup replaces saved data after you confirm. Keep any backup passcode somewhere safe, as Quiz Invoker cannot recover it.
